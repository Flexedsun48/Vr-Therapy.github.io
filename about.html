About

A fictional company with a human-centered mission.

LumaVive was created for this website project as a made-up health technology company focused on virtual reality exposure therapy.

Mission

Help clients approach feared situations with support, structure, and dignity.

Values

Clinical responsibility, gradual change, privacy, accessibility, and respect for client choice.

Team concept

The imagined team includes clinical psychologists, VR designers, accessibility specialists, privacy engineers, and client advocates. Their shared goal is to make exposure practice clear, adjustable, and less intimidating to begin.
