Preparing for home practice after VR exposure.

This third-level page demonstrates deeper linking while giving clients a clear, supportive guide for carrying session learning into daily life.

Before leaving the session

A good practice plan should be specific, realistic, and connected to the exposure completed in the clinic. The therapist and client choose one task that is challenging but not overwhelming.

  • Name the exact situation to approach.
  • Decide how long the practice will last.
  • Write down what coping behaviors to reduce.
  • Choose how progress will be recorded.

After practice

The client notes what happened, what they predicted, what they learned, and what step might come next. The goal is not to feel perfect; the goal is to build evidence that approach is possible.
