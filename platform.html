The platform

Clinical controls for immersive exposure work.

LumaVive combines VR scenes, session notes, biofeedback prompts, and therapist controls into one fictional care platform built for gradual exposure therapy.

Live intensity dial

Therapists can change distance, volume, crowd density, motion, lighting, and timing without ending the exercise.

Scenario builder

Clinics can assemble scenes from reusable modules such as elevators, balconies, checkout lines, bridges, and airports.

Session timeline

Client ratings, therapist observations, and exposure steps are captured as a simple progress record.

Designed for therapists

The clinician stays in charge.

The headset is only one part of care. LumaVive is imagined as a support tool for trained professionals who decide pacing, goals, and whether an exposure should continue.

  • Client consent prompts before every scenario change.
  • Safety plan reminders visible during intense moments.
  • Plain-language summaries for homework and follow-up.
