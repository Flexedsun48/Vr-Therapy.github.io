Clinician-led immersive care

LumaVive makes exposure therapy safer to start and easier to personalize.

Our fictional VR platform helps therapists guide clients through realistic, adjustable scenarios for anxiety, phobias, trauma-informed skill building, and everyday confidence.

Why VR exposure

Practice difficult moments without losing the safety of the room.

LumaVive gives clinicians a controlled way to introduce feared situations gradually. Every environment can be paused, reduced, repeated, or adjusted in real time based on the client’s goals.

Instead of asking a client to imagine a crowded train or wait weeks for a real flight, therapy can begin with a calm virtual version and grow from there.
