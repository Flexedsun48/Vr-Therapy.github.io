Social confidence

Rehearse conversations before they count.

The social confidence program imagines VR scenes for presentations, interviews, classroom participation, and casual conversations.

Practice without an audience of consequences.

Clients can try speaking, asking for help, making eye contact, or tolerating silence while the therapist adjusts the number of virtual people, tone of feedback, and length of interaction.

  • Interview room with supportive or neutral prompts.
  • Classroom question practice with adjustable audience size.
  • Small group conversation with turn-taking cues.
