Anxiety programs

Practice staying present when the body says leave.

The fictional anxiety program focuses on panic sensations, public spaces, transportation, and everyday uncertainty.

Program structure

Clients begin by naming avoided situations and rating how difficult each one feels. A therapist then selects a low-intensity VR scene, such as a quiet train platform or small waiting room, and increases complexity only when the client is ready.

  • Short exposures with reflection breaks.
  • Breathing and grounding cues used before and after immersion.
  • Progress notes that connect VR practice with real-world goals.
