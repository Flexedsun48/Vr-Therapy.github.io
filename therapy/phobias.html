Specific phobias

Small steps toward feared places, objects, and sensations.

VR can represent situations that are hard to arrange safely in an office, such as flying, heights, medical visits, and enclosed spaces.

Flights

Boarding, cabin sounds, takeoff motion, and turbulence can be separated into manageable scenes.

Heights

Balconies, glass elevators, bridges, and rooftops can start low and become more realistic.

Medical settings

Waiting rooms, exam chairs, and equipment sounds can be introduced before real appointments.

Why a module matters

A phobia is specific, but the person is not. LumaVive's fictional module pages show how content can be grouped by scenario while still letting clinicians personalize the pace.

Compare this with anxiety programs or see how progress is explained in outcomes.
